# Code of conduct

ShellCanvas should be a respectful, constructive and safe place for everyone who contributes, whatever their background or experience.

## What we expect

- Be considerate and assume good intent.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Accept correction gracefully, and give it kindly.
- Make room for different levels of experience; everyone was new once.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, threats or intimidation.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks.
- Sexualized language, imagery or attention.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or work.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Reports are handled as privately as practical.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments and other participation that break these expectations, and may restrict someone's participation temporarily or permanently.

## Scope

This policy applies in the project's repositories, issue trackers and review discussions, and in public spaces where someone represents ShellCanvas.
